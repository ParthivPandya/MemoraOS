## Limits

- 3 sources, one fact family (Python releases), small question count: this checks the mechanism on real dates, it is not a general benchmark.
- The sentences are templated renderings of real records. The unanswerable questions were written by the project.
- Mem0, Graphiti, Hindsight and Memorilla were not run.
