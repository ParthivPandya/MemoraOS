Generated by `python -m benchmarks.public`. Do not edit.

- Dataset: LongMemEval (longmemeval_s_cleaned.json), non-abstention questions

- Questions: 60
- Embedder: hashing; retrieval unit: session (chunked to 800 chars)
- This measures retrieval only. It is not the dataset's answer-accuracy metric and is not comparable to published QA scores.
